The first release.

- Nintendo's Donkey Kong Jr. (1982), from MAME's `dkongjr` set in `/DKONGJR` on the card.
- The arcade's Z80 program and its 8035 sound program run as they do in MAME 0.288, with the sound circuits after the 8035.
- The play area at three quarters size, with the scores, bonus, level and lives at full size in the margins.
- Coin on keypad \* or #, one player on 1, two on 2. USB pads work too.
- Works with the cart's current firmware.
